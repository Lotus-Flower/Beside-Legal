Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026  ·  Effective: May 10, 2026

Beside is a pet journaling app built to celebrate time spent with the animals you love. We collect only what's necessary to make the app work and keep your memories safe. We don't sell your data, show you ads, or track you across the web.

1. Who We Are

Beside is an independent app developed and operated by an individual developer based in Fulton County, Georgia, USA. Questions about this policy can be directed to besidepet@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Email address — used for account creation and sign-in via Firebase Authentication
  • Pet information — name, species, breed, and photos you enter into the app
  • Moment logs — activities, notes, and photos you log within the app
  • Payment information — if you subscribe to Beside Premium, payment is handled entirely by RevenueCat and Apple/Google billing. We never see or store your card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Account identifiers — a unique user ID assigned by Firebase when you create an account
  • App usage data — basic information about how you interact with the app, used to improve the experience
  • Crash and performance data — if the app crashes or encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects diagnostic information including device model, operating system version, crash stack traces, and the state of the app at the time of the crash. This data does not include personal content such as photos or journal entries.

Device permissions

  • Camera — only accessed when you choose to take a photo
  • Photo library — only accessed when you choose to upload a photo

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver and operate the Beside app and its features
  • To back up your pet journals and moments to the cloud (when signed in)
  • To process subscription payments via RevenueCat
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To send administrative communications (e.g. policy updates)
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and performance issues (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • To evaluate and improve our app and user experience
  • To protect the security and integrity of our services

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising or marketing purposes. We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Google / Firebase — authentication (Firebase Auth), database (Firestore), photo storage (Cloud Storage), and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics). Google's Data Processing Terms apply. Learn more.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our active systems. Some information may be retained for a short period in backup systems before permanent deletion.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored using Firebase, which provides encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no system is 100% secure. Use the app within a secure network environment.

7. Children's Privacy

Beside is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at besidepet@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to withdraw consent to processing
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at besidepet@gmail.com.

9. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, email besidepet@gmail.com.

Categories of personal information collected in the past 12 months: identifiers (email address, user ID), visual data (photos you upload), and financial information (processed by RevenueCat on our behalf).

10. Do Not Track

We do not respond to Do Not Track signals. We do not use tracking technologies such as cookies, web beacons, or advertising pixels.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice in the app before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
